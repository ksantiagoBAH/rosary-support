Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Rosary is an offline prayer app provided by Kenneth Santiago.

Data collection

Rosary does not collect or transmit your personal information, prayer activity, or device identifiers to the developer. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Saved on your device

Your selected narration voice and saved prayer position are stored locally so you can resume prayer. All prayer text, recordings, artwork, and animations are bundled with the app. Audio plays on your device, including background and lock-screen playback. The widget calculates its daily mysteries and rotating meditation on your device.

Narration and third parties

Narration was generated in advance with ElevenLabs and is included as prerecorded audio. The app does not connect to ElevenLabs or send it your information. Rosary does not share your saved prayer information with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences and saved prayer progress remain until replaced or until you delete the app using Delete App in iOS Settings. Offloading the app may keep its data. Device backups may retain app data according to your Apple backup settings; manage those backups through Apple. The developer has no copy of your local prayer progress to retrieve or delete.

Support

If you email rosaryappsupport@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to include. This is used to answer your request, retained only as needed for support, and is not used for advertising. Email is handled by the email providers involved. To request deletion of support correspondence, email the same address. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Apple services

App Store, device backups, and any diagnostics you choose to share with Apple are governed by Apple's policies and your device settings.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Contact rosaryappsupport@gmail.com with privacy questions.
