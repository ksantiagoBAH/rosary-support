Support for Rosary, a free offline iPhone and iPad app for the Holy Rosary and Divine Mercy Chaplet.

Contact support

Email rosaryappsupport@gmail.com with your app version, device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Prayer and audio

Prayer text, recordings, and artwork are included in the app. Choose a narration voice in Audio options. Your prayer progress is saved on your device. Background narration and lock-screen controls are available during playback.

Widget help

Add Rosary Today from the Home Screen widget gallery. It shows the day's mysteries and highlights a meditation on a three-hour schedule. That highlight is independent of saved prayer progress. If a stale snapshot remains after an update, open Rosary, then remove and re-add the widget.

Support development

Rosary is free, with no ads or in-app purchases. If it helps you find a moment of peace, you can buy me a coffee to support its development and future improvements. Your prayers and encouragement mean just as much.

Privacy

Rosary has no advertising, tracking, or accounts. Read the privacy policy for local-storage and support-email details.
